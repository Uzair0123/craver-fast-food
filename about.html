Sizzle & Passion

Born In An Open-Flame Kitchen

Founded in 2021, Craver Fast Food Co. began as a humble smash burger food truck with a mission to deliver gourmet quality at everyday neighborhood prices.

We smash our 100% prime Angus beef patties on seasoned cast iron griddles to achieve the crispiest lacy edges while locking in every drop of savory juice. Every brioche bun is toasted in clarified farm butter, and our signature sauces are prepared fresh every single morning.
