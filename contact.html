Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding deliveries, dietary choices, and payment.

What is the standard delivery radius & time?
We deliver within an 8-mile radius of our kitchen hubs. Average delivery time is 25 to 30 minutes, delivered in thermal heat-insulated carriers.
Are your burger patties 100% fresh?
Yes! We never freeze our Angus beef patties. They are ground fresh daily, hand-smashed on high-heat cast iron griddles for crispy caramelized edges.
Do you offer vegetarian and spicy options?
Absolutely. Use our menu filters to view 100% vegetarian options (like our Halloumi Burger, Truffle Fries, and Four-Cheese Pizza) or spicy hits like our Nashville Hot Tenders.
How can I track my order or apply a coupon?
You can enter promo codes like CRAVE40 or CRAVEFREE directly in your shopping bag before proceeding to checkout.
